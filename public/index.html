FAQs

Questions, answered

Who can register?

Students enrolled in the programmes listed under Eligibility. You need a valid college ID to receive your certificate.

Can I join as a team?

Yes. A team of 3 to 4 students pays one fee of ₹299. You can also enter solo for ₹99.

What does the entry fee cover?

It covers your seat, access to problem statements and mentors, evaluation and your certificate. It is not a guarantee of any prize or job.

When does registration open?

Registration opens on 1 November 2026 and closes on 20 December 2026.

Will Scalefox help with internships and placement?

Yes. We connect strong participants with internship and placement opportunities through our network. Selection is made by the hiring partners, so it is not guaranteed for everyone.

Can I change my track later?

You can switch until registration closes. After that, tracks are locked so each leaderboard stays fair.

Can I use AI tools while building?

Yes, as assistants. You must understand, explain and be able to defend everything you submit, and you must say which tools you used.

Where are the official rules?

The full rules will be published on this site before registration opens, along with the code of conduct and the refund policy.
