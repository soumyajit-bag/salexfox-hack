FAQs

Questions, answered

Who can register?

Students from B.Tech, B.E., BCA, B.Sc., MCA, M.Sc., MBA, Diploma, and other tech enthusiast & builder communities are welcome.

Can I join as a team?

Yes. Participate individually or in a team of 3-4 members. Team participation is ₹299 per team, while solo participation is ₹99.

What does the entry fee cover?

It covers your seat, access to problem statements and mentors, evaluation, and your participation certificate. It is not a guarantee of any prize, internship, or job.

When does registration open?

Registration starts on 3rd November 2026 and closes on 10th December 2026.

Will ScaleFox help with internships and placement?

Yes. Strong performing participants may be shortlisted for internship interviews and connected with hiring partners. Selection depends on openings, eligibility, and hiring partner decisions.

Can I change my track later?

You can select your track during registration. Once the hackathon begins, track choices are fixed to keep evaluation fair.

Can I use AI tools while building?

Yes, as assistants, but you must understand, explain, and defend everything you submit. You should also be prepared to disclose the tools you used.

Where are the official rules?

The official hackathon rules and code of conduct will be shared before the event, along with additional participation details.
